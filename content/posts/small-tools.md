There's a particular joy in a tool that does one thing well. You learn it once and then you forget it exists, which is the highest compliment software can get.

## Three signs of a good small tool

1. It starts instantly.
2. It has fewer settings than you expected.
3. You can explain it to a friend in one sentence.

Small tools compose. A big tool asks you to live inside it; a small tool comes to where you already are.
